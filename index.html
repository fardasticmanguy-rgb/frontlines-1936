HOW TO PLAY

• Everyone plans at the same time, then all orders happen at once.

• Drag from your province onto a target to send every free division there. On a computer you can also click your province and right-click the target (Shift + right-click sends half). Tap your province to fine-tune the count with − / + / ½ / ALL.

• Attack any neighboring province, or redeploy up to 4 provinces through your own land in a single turn. Blue arrows are redeployments, red arrows are attacks.

• Select many: Shift-drag a box or Ctrl-click provinces, then right-click (or tap) a target to send them all at once.

• No dice. The bigger army wins and keeps the difference. Defenders get bonuses: forest ×1.25, mountains ×1.5, fort +0.5, capital +0.25.

• Two armies attacking each other meet in the middle and fight there.

• Supply: provinces must connect through your land to one of your cities. Cut-off provinces are hatched, defend at ×0.75 and lose divisions every turn. Encircle your enemies!

• Winter (Dec–Feb) freezes the front: every attack is 20% weaker.

• Each nation has a trait: blitzkrieg (+10% attack), cheaper factories, free divisions in captured cities, fortified cities, immunity to winter, or tougher defense in forests and mountains. Tap a flag to see it.

• Diplomacy: tap a flag in the top bar. Offer a pact, invite nations into your faction or ask to join theirs. A pact forms once both sides have offered. Faction members can't attack each other, share supply lines, fight as one army and win together, but need more victory points: 75% for two, 85% for three. You can also send aid (industry) to anyone, or leave your faction.

• Play on this device lets you pick the map, 2 to 8 nations, map size, AI difficulty, winter and events. Tap several nations to make them players for pass-and-play.

• Europe 1939 drops you into September 1939 with the real powers. Dashed lines are sea crossings you can attack across.

• World events like strikes, booms and partisan uprisings can shake up the war.

• Each turn you earn ⚙ industry. Open 🏭 PRODUCE (or press P) to spend it across all your cities: infantry (1 ⚙), tanks (2 ⚙), forts (6) and factories. Your production repeats every turn until you change it; AUTO fills it for you.

• Tanks 🛡 hit ×3 on plains, ×2 in forests and ×1.25 in mountains, but defend at only ×1.5. Infantry soak up casualties first. Each factory level lets a city build 2 tanks per turn.

• Factories upgrade up to level 3 (8 / 12 / 16 ⚙) and each level yields +3 ⚙ every turn. A captured factory loses a level, so raid your enemy's industry!

• Cities ■ are worth 1 victory point and capitals ★ are worth 3. Hit the VP goal, be the last nation left, or lead when the final month ends.

• Pinch or scroll to zoom. Drag empty map (or right/middle-drag, or two fingers) to pan. Tap ⤢ or press F to zoom back out.

• Keys: WASD / arrows pan · Q / E zoom · Space ends the turn · Z or Ctrl+Z undo · X clears the selected orders · P production · Esc deselects.
